Our Story

One fire, one table, whoever's hungry.

Asha Restaurant started as a single fire pit and a borrowed grill. The idea stayed simple: cook everything over open flame, source what's in season, and put it all on the table to share.

The kitchen changes with the market — some nights it's tilapia straight off the coast, other nights it's whatever the garden gave up that morning. What doesn't change is the fire.
